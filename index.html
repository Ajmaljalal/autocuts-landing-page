Questions about Autocuts

Which Macs does Autocuts support?

Autocuts is built for Apple Silicon Macs. macOS Sonoma 14.0 or later is recommended.

Do my recordings leave my Mac?

No. Recordings are written to the Videos folder on your Mac as normal files. Transcription runs on your device, so nothing is uploaded.

Can Autocuts record system audio?

Yes. Autocuts can capture system audio together with your microphone and camera on supported macOS versions.

What can Autocuts export?

MP4 (H.264) or WebM (VP9), up to 4K at 60 fps, with optional burned-in captions plus SRT and VTT sidecar files.

Is Autocuts a subscription?

No. Autocuts is a one-time purchase. You keep the app and your files.

Can I change the video after I stop recording?

Yes. The studio lets you edit zooms, the cursor, camera layouts, backgrounds, clips, speed, and captions after the take.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Autocuts is not for you, write within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.

How do I contact support?

Email support@autocuts.ai. A human reads every message.
