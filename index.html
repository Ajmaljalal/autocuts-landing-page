Answers about recording on a Mac

What is the best Mac screen recorder with automatic zoom?

Look for local capture, zooms you can edit after recording, smooth cursor motion, captions, and 4K export. Autocuts covers all five on Apple Silicon Macs with a one-time $34.99 license, which makes it a strong pick for product demos and tutorials.

Which Macs does Autocuts support?

Autocuts runs on Apple Silicon Macs and works best on macOS Sonoma 14 or newer.

Do my recordings leave my Mac?

No. Recordings and project data stay in Autocuts on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded for editing or export.

Does Autocuts capture system audio?

Yes. Autocuts can capture system audio together with your microphone and camera on supported macOS versions.
