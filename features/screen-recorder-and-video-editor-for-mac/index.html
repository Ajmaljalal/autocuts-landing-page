What is Autocuts?

Autocuts is an Apple Silicon screen recorder and video editor built for software demos, tutorials, courses, YouTube videos, onboarding, support, and launch content. It combines high-quality capture with automatic editing and manual control.

Record complete source material

Capture more than a flat video file

Autocuts records the picture plus click, cursor, key, and voice data. That gives the editor enough context to generate useful zooms, reconstruct smooth cursor movement, and align captions or graphics with the narration.

  • Display, app window, or selected area
  • Camera, microphone, and system audio
  • Global recording shortcuts and teleprompter

Timing and pace

Let the editor start the work

Clicks become editable camera moves. Cursor points become a smoother path. Speech becomes a transcript and captions. For motion graphics, add an item manually or let a supported coding agent propose moments from the transcript.

Can I record and edit a screen video in the same Mac app?

Yes. The project opens in the editor after the take, with recorded media and capture data still connected. You do not need to render a raw video and rebuild the project in a separate editor.

What can I edit after recording?

You can trim and split clips, change speed, edit zooms and cursor effects, arrange camera layouts, add backgrounds, clean the transcript, style captions, and add motion graphics.

What can I export?

Export MP4 H.264 or WebM VP9 up to 4K at 60 fps. Captions can be burned into the picture or delivered as SRT and VTT files.

Who is it for?

Autocuts fits people explaining software: founders making SaaS or app demos, product marketers preparing a launch, developers walking through a change, teachers recording a tutorial, and creators publishing screen-first videos.
