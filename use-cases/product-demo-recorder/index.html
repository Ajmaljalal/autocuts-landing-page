Make the important click obvious

Small interface controls disappear when a wide desktop recording is embedded on a landing page or watched on a phone. Autocuts uses each click to create a deliberate camera move around the active area.

Add a background, padding, rounded corners, shadow, and camera layout after recording. The product remains readable while the video matches your brand.

A simple product demo workflow

1

Plan one clear outcome

Choose the feature, starting state, three to five important actions, and the final result the viewer should understand.

2

Record the path

Capture the app, narration, and optional camera. Use the teleprompter when the wording needs to stay precise.

3

Polish and repurpose

Trim pauses, refine zooms, add captions, branding, and a useful motion graphic, then export for your site or social post.

What makes a product demo effective?

Lead with the result, keep the path short, and let each visual change earn its place. A viewer should understand what changed and why it matters without reading every label on the screen.

Automatic zoom is useful for attention, but too many moves can become distracting. Keep the generated zooms that clarify a control and remove those that interrupt the larger view.

Where can you use the exported demo?

Autocuts can produce a standard MP4 for a landing page, product launch, documentation, help center, changelog, or sales message. Square and portrait canvases help adapt the same workflow for social posts.

Can I include my camera?

Yes. Record screen and camera together, then choose a bubble, split, stack, or solo layout in the editor.
