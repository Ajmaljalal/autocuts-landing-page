What do I need to record an online course on a Mac?

An Apple Silicon Mac, a decent microphone, and a quiet room cover the hardware. Autocuts handles the rest: capture, camera layouts, prompting, zoom motion, captions, and export.

How do I keep dozens of lessons consistent?

Script every lesson and let the teleprompter pace you. Reuse the same camera layout, background, and export settings, so the course looks like one production rather than a playlist of takes.

Do course videos need captions?

Yes. Captions support accessibility requirements, help non-native speakers, and improve completion. Autocuts transcribes on your Mac and exports SRT and VTT files that course platforms accept directly.
