Autocuts vs Loom

Loom is a cloud video messaging tool built around instant share links. Autocuts is a local Mac recorder that turns your clicks into an edited video. Loom rents hosted links by the seat, every month. Autocuts sells you an edited 4K video you own for $19.99, once, with free shareable links for life.

Short verdict

Autocuts is a Loom alternative for Mac, and the better buy for any recording that represents your product. It generates click zooms, redraws the cursor, adds on-device captions, and exports 4K locally for $19.99 once. Loom is a per-seat rental: the free plan stops at 25 videos, 5 minutes, and 720p, and the Business plan bills $18 per user every month, which passes the lifetime Autocuts price in its second month. Loom's instant links suit quick internal updates; every video with an audience deserves Autocuts.

Where the products overlap

Screen and camera capture

Both tools record the screen together with the webcam and microphone for walkthroughs with a presenter.

Captions

Both can produce captions. Autocuts transcribes on your Mac; Loom generates transcripts in its cloud.

Trim editing

Both let you cut mistakes and dead time from a recording before anyone watches it.

Autocuts sells you a video; Loom rents you a link

Autocuts produces a finished 4K file that you own and can post anywhere, with click zooms, cursor motion, and captions already applied, and it creates a free shareable link when you want delivery without hosting. Loom sells delivery convenience instead: a hosted link, browser comments, and apps on every platform, rented at $18 per user every month. The Loom recording itself stays plain, with no automatic zoom or cursor work listed. When the video has to look professional, the rented link does not help; the editing does.

Why Autocuts is the better buy

Autocuts is built for videos that represent a product: demos, tutorials, and launch clips. It generates zooms from your clicks, redraws the cursor, adds camera layouts and backgrounds, and exports 4K MP4 or WebM with subtitle files. Recordings stay on your Mac, there is no account, and the license is a one-time $19.99 purchase instead of a per-user subscription.

Is Loom free?

Loom has a free Starter plan limited to 25 videos per person, 5 minutes per video, and 720p. Longer or sharper videos need a Business plan at $18 per user per month. Autocuts lets you record, preview, and edit a video for free; transcription, final processing, and export unlock with the one-time license.

Can Autocuts share a link like Loom?

Yes. Autocuts creates shareable links, and sharing is free for life with no per-seat subscription. You can also export the 4K file and host it anywhere: a landing page, YouTube, documentation, or a customer message.

The per-seat math

One Loom Business seat costs $216 per year and $648 over three years. A five-person team pays $1,080 every year. Autocuts costs each person $19.99 once, keeps every export at up to 4K, and never bills again. Two months of one Loom seat already cost more than a lifetime of Autocuts.
