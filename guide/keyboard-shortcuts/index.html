Keyboard shortcuts.

Control every recording without touching the recorder pill. These shortcuts work system-wide while Autocuts runs.

TL;DR

Option+1 starts or stops a screen recording, Option+2 pauses and resumes, Option+3 records a selected area, and Option+4 stops and keeps the take. Control+Option+Space opens the screen switcher on multi-display Macs.

Global shortcuts

ActionShortcut

Choose the recorded screenControl + Option + Space
Record the next screenControl + Option + Right
Record the previous screenControl + Option + Left

How does the screen switcher work?

With the switcher open, the left and right arrow keys highlight a display, the space bar switches the recording to it, Return confirms, and Escape cancels. The recording keeps running while you switch.

Where can I see the current shortcuts?

The Autocuts settings window lists every registered global shortcut, so you can check the bindings active on your Mac at a glance.
