How to use Autocuts.

Short, practical pages that cover the whole workflow: install the app, record your screen, edit the automatic zooms, and export with captions.

Get started

Setup

Install Autocuts on your Mac

Download the DMG, move the app into Applications, and grant the macOS permissions.

 Setup

System requirements

Apple Silicon, macOS Sonoma 14 or later, and enough disk space for local 4K recordings.

 First take

Record your screen, a window, or an area

Use the recorder pill and the capture frame, then start with a click or a shortcut.

 First take

Keyboard shortcuts

Start, pause, stop, and switch screens without touching the recorder pill.

Record

Audio

Record system audio on a Mac

Capture app sound and your microphone together, with no virtual audio driver.

 Camera

Use the ten camera layouts

From a floating bubble to a full portrait panel, plus vertical video for Shorts and Reels.

 Script

Use the voice-paced teleprompter

Read a script that follows your voice and never appears in the recording.

Edit and export

Zooms

Edit the automatic zooms

Review the zoom blocks Autocuts created, adjust them, add your own, or delete them.

 Cursor

Change the cursor size, color, and motion

Increase the cursor size, pick a color, and smooth the path after recording.

 Transcript

Clean up a recording with the transcript

Transcribe on your Mac, then cut filler words and sections by editing text.

 Export

Export video and captions

MP4 or WebM up to 4K at 60 fps, with SRT and VTT caption files next to the video.

Sharing

Create a share link

Turn a finished video into a free link that plays from your own Google Drive.
